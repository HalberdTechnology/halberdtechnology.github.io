MOONBLUFF

Someone is lying

Everyone is dealt a card. The cards wake in turn and some of them swap others around in the dark. Then the table has one day to talk and a single hidden vote to find a wolf.

One night, one day, one vote

Why it plays well

Built for a table of phones

Nobody gives it away

Every role gets a turn, even one lying in the middle, and every phone has something to tap. You can't tell who's acting from a neighbour's screen.

In person or remote

Around one table, phones down at dawn and the arguing done out loud. Or apart on a call, with claims made in the app.

One game, ten minutes

One night and one day. No eliminations, nobody sitting out: everyone plays to the vote.

Pick the roles

The host turns optional roles on or off in the lobby, so the night is as simple or as twisted as the table wants.

Practice against bots

Learn the night order and the bluffs on your own before you bring them to the table.

Native and fast

Written in Rust on the Halberd engine, the same one under Revolution.

The night

Who wakes, and what they do

Wolf

Wolf team. Sees the pack. Two are always in.

Hound

Wolf team. Sniffs one player: do they hold the Oracle?

Saboteur

Wolf team. Jams one player: tonight's reads on them come out wrong.

Oracle

Town. Learns whether one player's card is on the wolf team.

Gossip

Town. Learns whether two players are on the same team.

Bodyguard

Town. Guards one card: it can't move tonight.

Smuggler

Town. Swaps a player's card with one in the middle, unseen.

Forger

Town. Swaps their own card with another player's, unseen.

Lookout

Town. Learns whether a player's card has moved.

Judge

Town. By day, their vote counts twice.

Plus the Accomplice, Archivist, Sleepwalker, Witness and plain Citizens. As many cards as players, plus three face down in the middle.
