How it works

Out loud, around real life

Start a room

Get a six-letter code and send it to friends. A room lasts until its last member leaves; nothing expires.

Slip it in

Say your phrase in a real conversation with people from that room, in person or on a call. Texts and voice notes don't count.

Score it

Easy phrases are worth 1 point, medium 2, hard 3. Tap I slipped it in and the next one is dealt straight away.

The feed

Every slipped phrase shows up in the room's feed, so everyone finds out what was snuck past them.

Never the same phrase

Each phrase is dealt once per room, and never twice at once across your rooms. Stuck with one for a week? Skip it.

Spicy, if you want

Off by default. A room's owner can turn on cheeky, adult phrases for the friends who'd enjoy them.
