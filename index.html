Revolution

A bluffing card game

Every player holds two hidden roles and nobody has to tell the truth about them. Claim a power you don't have, call out a liar, and be the last one with a card left. Built for road trips: every player on their own phone.

Moonbluff

Someone is lying

A one-night hidden-role game for a table of phones. Cards wake in turn and some of them swap others around in the dark; then the table gets one day to talk and a single hidden vote to find a wolf. Every phone taps every turn, so nobody can read a power off a neighbour's screen.

iPhone3 to 10 playersIn person or remotePractice with bots

Under the Radar

Say it without getting clocked

Everyone always holds one secret phrase. Work it into a real, spoken conversation without anyone catching on, tick it off, get the next. Rooms with your friends last for months: the flat, work, family. Texts don't count.
