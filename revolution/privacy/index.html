Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

The short version
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The callsign you type and your moves are passed between players during a game, then forgotten.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.
  • We never sell or share your data.

This policy covers the Revolution app for iPhone (the "App"), made by Halberd Technology ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App handles and why.

What the App handles

InformationWhyWhere it goes
CallsignShown to the other players in your game.Sent through our relay server to the players in your room. You choose it, and it does not need to be your real name.
Game messagesYour actions, challenges and blocks, so everyone sees the same table.Passed through the relay server in real time and not stored once the game ends.
Room codeLets friends join the right game.Held by the relay server only while the room is open.
IP addressNeeded for any internet connection to work.Seen by the relay server while you are connected. Our web server may keep standard connection logs (IP address and time) for a short period to keep the service secure and working.
SettingsRemembering light or dark mode and whether sound is on.Saved in a small file on your phone. Never sent to us.

What the App does not do

  • It does not ask for or create an account, email address or phone number.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, or any third-party tracking SDKs.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Local network

iOS may ask to let Revolution find devices on your local network. This is used only to connect directly to other players' phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

Sharing

Your callsign and game moves are visible to the other players in your game, because that is how the game works. We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Retention

Game data exists only for the length of a game. Short-lived server connection logs are deleted on a rolling basis. Settings stay on your phone until you delete the App.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. As the App collects no identifying information, there is nothing for us to delete, but please contact us if you have a concern.

Security

Connections to our relay server are encrypted with standard TLS.

Your choices

You can use any callsign you like, and you can delete all data the App keeps on your phone by deleting the App. Because we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is no account to close.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
