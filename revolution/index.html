Why it plays well

Built for the back seat

One phone each

Your hand lives on your screen and nowhere else. One phone hosts, the others join with a short room code.

Practice against bots

Learn the bluffs on your own before the road trip, then bring them to the table.

Quick rounds

Ten minutes a game. The rules fit on one card and the arguments last the whole drive.

No accounts, no ads

Pick a callsign and play. Nothing to sign up for and nothing tracking you.

Light and dark

A clean monochrome table with one cyan accent, hand-drawn card art and synthesized sound.

Native and fast

Written in Rust with custom shaders. A small download that runs smoothly.

The court

Five roles. Claim any of them.

Magnate

Levy: take 3 coins. Blocks foreign aid.

Operative

Eliminate: pay 3, a target loses a card.

Enforcer

Extort: take 2 coins from a player. Blocks extortion.

Broker

Swap: draw 2, keep the best. Blocks extortion.

Sentinel

Blocks an elimination aimed at you.

Save up seven coins and you can Purge any player, no questions asked. Get caught lying and you lose a card. Lose both and you are out.

Support

Getting a game going

  1. Everyone installs Revolution and picks a callsign.
  2. One player taps Host game and reads out the room code.
  3. Everyone else taps Join game and types the code.
  4. The host starts the round once everyone is in.

Questions

Do we need internet?

Yes. Online games connect through our relay server, so each phone needs mobile data or Wi-Fi. A steady connection gives the best game, so expect the odd hiccup in tunnels.

How many people can play?

Two to six. You can also play solo against bots.

Why does it ask for local network access?

So phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot can find each other directly. It is only used to connect to other players' phones.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Revolution is iPhone-only for now.

What does the app collect?

Nothing that identifies you. The details are in the privacy policy.
