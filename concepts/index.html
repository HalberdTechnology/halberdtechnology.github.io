Halberd Technology

Landing page concepts

Three halberd-led directions mixing the K-ov hero with Revolution's look, built for a studio with many games. Whichever is picked becomes the home page.

  1. A / HaloThe K-ov hero almost as-is: a full halberd stands behind a giant centered wordmark, with the Vanta halo ringing its blade in cyan. Games sit in a glass "armoury" panel below.
  2. B / GlintA halberd cuts diagonally across a custom smoke shader, a light glint runs along its steel, and a cyan rim light follows the pointer. Games hang on "the rack" below.
  3. C / ArmouryThe halberd is the page: its head crowns the hero, the shaft splits HAL / BERD and runs down the whole site, filling with cyan as you scroll, and each game hangs off it like a notch.
